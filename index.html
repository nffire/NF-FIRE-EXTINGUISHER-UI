  Class B Fire Protection

Automatic Foam Fire Suppression

Instant Control. Absolute Protection.

Petrol pumps, fuel yards, and storage depots demand split-second responses to liquid fuel fires. Our Automatic Foam Release Suppression Systems deploy Aqueous Film Forming Foam (AFFF) the moment a threat is detected — smothering flames, suppressing vapors, and preventing re-ignition, all without human intervention.

  • Instant Thermal Trigger Thermal sensors detect abnormal heat and activate the suppression system in under 10 seconds.
  • AFFF — Aqueous Film Forming Foam Forms an aqueous film that seals the fuel surface, cutting off oxygen and halting the combustion process instantly.
  • High Expansion Coverage Foam expands up to 1000× its volume — providing blanket coverage across wide fuel spill areas rapidly.
  • No Manual Intervention Required Fully automated systems protect unmanned fuel stations 24/7, even when no personnel are present.
